# Privacy

This app stores preferences, addon configurations and viewing progress in its application storage on the TV. If you connect a Stremio account, the TV keeps a session and a local copy of supported library and viewing data. Use **Settings → Stremio account → Sign out** to remove the session and imported library data and request session revocation. Local preferences, installed addons and TV viewing progress stay on the TV.

The app contacts your selected addon services and media providers. Stremio sync reads your library, watched markers and resume positions. If you connect Trakt, the app can send resume positions, viewing events, manual watched marks and ratings for eligible titles to Trakt. Torrent playback communicates with trackers and peers. Your public IP address is visible to peers you connect to.

When reporting a problem, share only the TV model, Tizen version, app version and steps you took. Remove account details, addon credentials, playlist URLs and device identifiers from any screenshots or logs.
